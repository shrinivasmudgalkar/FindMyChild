FindMyChild

A child-safety reporting prototype to help organize missing-child and found-child reports for authorized review.

Important: If a child is missing or found, contact local police or emergency services promptly. This demo does not notify authorities or confirm a child's identity.

Report a Found Child

Possible Matches

Potential matches are based on approximate age and general area. A match is not proof of identity.

This demo does not use facial recognition. In a real system, potential matches must be reviewed by authorized child-protection personnel.
