Safety & Privacy

This is an educational prototype. Do not upload real children's photos or enter real names, exact locations, school details, or contact information.

Before real-world deployment

The system would require secure storage, verified access, audit logs, retention rules, and legal and child-safeguarding review. Any potential match must be reviewed by authorized professionals.
